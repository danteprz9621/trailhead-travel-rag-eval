Trailhead Travel Visa & Entry Requirements

Visa requirements depend on the traveler's nationality, destination, and purpose of travel, and Trailhead Travel does not issue visas or make entry determinations on a customer's behalf. We strongly recommend checking the destination country's official immigration or embassy website at least 60 days before departure, since requirements can change with little notice.

Passports must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the planned return date for most international destinations; several countries (including many in the Schengen Area) will deny boarding for passports that do not meet this rule even if the visa itself is valid. Some destinations additionally require at least two blank visa pages.

Trailhead Travel is not responsible for denied boarding or denied entry resulting from invalid, expired, or insufficient travel documents, and no refund is issued in these cases under the standard refund policy — only travelers with an active Trailhead Travel Insurance policy that explicitly includes "documentation and visa denial" coverage may be eligible for reimbursement.

Transit passengers connecting through a third country on a Trailhead-booked itinerary may still need a transit visa even if they do not leave the airport; this is most common on itineraries connecting through the UK, Australia, and Canada. Trailhead's booking confirmation displays a general transit visa advisory, but travelers must confirm exact requirements with the relevant embassy.
