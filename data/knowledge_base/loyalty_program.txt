Trailhead Rewards Loyalty Program

Trailhead Rewards has four tiers based on qualifying miles flown per calendar year: Blue (0 miles, default), Silver (25,000 miles), Gold (50,000 miles), and Platinum (100,000 miles). Tier status is evaluated on a rolling 12-month basis and resets if the qualifying threshold isn't met again within that window, though members are given a 3-month grace period before downgrade.

Silver members receive priority check-in and free same-day standby (see booking_changes.txt). Gold and Platinum members receive one additional free checked bag on every flight (see baggage_policy.txt) and complimentary seat upgrades to Premium Economy when available at check-in. Platinum members additionally receive complimentary Business Class upgrades on flights under 5 hours, subject to availability, and are exempt from the $75 standard change fee described in booking_changes.txt regardless of fare class.

Points earned through flights, hotel stays, and the Trailhead co-branded credit card do not expire as long as the account has at least one qualifying transaction every 18 months; otherwise, all accumulated points are forfeited. Points cannot be transferred between accounts except in the case of a documented account holder's death, and cannot be redeemed for cash, gift cards, or the "cancel for any reason" travel insurance premium described in travel_insurance.txt.
